Enrollment Steps for New Students

How to Join Us:

  1. Submit your online application via the Student Portal.
  2. Provide official high school transcripts and recommendation letters.
  3. Schedule a virtual or in-person campus tour.
  4. Receive your admission decision and complete registration.
